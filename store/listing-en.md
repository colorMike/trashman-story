# Store listing (English)

**Name:** Trashman Story – We feed datakrakens

**Summary (132 chars max):**
Dress up your Trashmen. They surf the web for you and feed ad trackers with data trash that makes your profile worthless.

**Description:**

You are being followed. After half an hour of normal browsing, dozens of
advertising networks have stored a profile of you: what you looked at, what
you might buy, where you live.

Most privacy tools try to hide you. Trashman Story does the opposite. It sends
three Trashmen out to surf the web for you. You dress them in outfits: a
Bavarian, a Nerd and a Cook, for example. Each outfit is a topic. The Trashmen
search for it, open shops, click through results and leave a trail of data
trash. The advertising networks happily eat it and sell the result. Enjoy the
Lederhosen ads.

* Pick three outfits from 51, grouped in Cultures, Animals, Hobbies and Professions.
* Flip the switch. The Trashmen work in their own pinned, muted tab.
* Human-like pacing, pauses while you are away, daily limit.
* See what they did: pages per day, per outfit, last visited pages.
* Check the effect yourself in your Google, Facebook or Amazon ad settings.

No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Everything stays in your browser.
Open source under the MIT licence.

Trashman Story started as a master project at the University of Applied
Sciences Salzburg, Multimedia Technology, in 2014. This is version 2.
